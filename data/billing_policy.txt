Billing Policy

Subscription charges occur on the first day of each billing cycle.

Refund Requests:
- Refund requests are reviewed within 3 business days.
- Duplicate charges require manual investigation.
- Refund approvals may take 5 to 7 business days.

Billing Disputes:
- Users must provide transaction details.
- Support agents may request payment receipts.

Failed Payments:
- Users should verify card information.
- Ensure sufficient account balance.
